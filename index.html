Audio sync

Palette

With "Tab/screen audio", pick the tab playing the music (or the entire screen on Windows) and tick Share audio.

Remap

Click a key in the drawing to choose what it does.

Layout

Keys with a colored outline differ from the factory default.

Macros are saved over the USB-C cable. After that the keyboard types them by itself, on any computer.

Text macros

How it works

Each macro replaces what a key on the base layer does. The text is typed as on a US keyboard: unaccented letters, numbers and ASCII symbols. Removing a macro resets the key to its default.

Sleep

According to AULA, sleep only applies in wireless mode.

Getting started

  1. Plug in the F75 with the USB-C cable (recommended) or the 2.4G receiver.
  2. Click Connect keyboard and pick "AULA F75" / "Compx" in the browser dialog.
  3. Done: every change goes straight to the keyboard and stays stored on it, even without this site.

Trouble?

  • Close AULA's official driver if it is open.
  • On 2.4G, press a key to wake the keyboard and try again.
  • Linux: the browser needs access to hidraw. Run once: 
    sudo tee /etc/udev/rules.d/70-aula-f75.rules <<'EOF'
SUBSYSTEM=="hidraw", ATTRS{idVendor}=="3554", ATTRS{idProduct}=="fa09", TAG+="uaccess"
SUBSYSTEM=="hidraw", ATTRS{idVendor}=="258a", ATTRS{idProduct}=="010c", TAG+="uaccess"
EOF
sudo udevadm control --reload-rules && sudo udevadm trigger
    then replug the cable or the receiver.
  • Profiles are stored in this browser. Use Export/Import to take them to another computer.
